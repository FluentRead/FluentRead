## Translation Center: compare services

Open **Settings Center → Tools and learning → Translation Center**, enter text, choose languages and click **Translate** (⌘ + Enter on Mac, Ctrl + Enter elsewhere).

[Open guide](/en/guide/translation-center)

## Section translation

When you only want to read part of a page, such as a GitHub README, an article body or one comment thread, you don’t have to translate the whole page.

[Open guide](/en/guide/webpage-translation)

## English word lookup

The selection card includes 3,000 common English dictionary entries, available offline without a separate download.

[Open guide](/en/guide/deepseek-harness)

## Translation style

Choose how translations look in bilingual mode under **Settings → Interface style → Translation style**.

[Open guide](/en/config/appearance)

## Bilingual sentence highlighting

Enable sentence highlighting and point to a sentence on either side to highlight its counterpart. It helps locate a difficult sentence in a long paragraph.

[Open guide](/en/config/appearance)

## Selection translation

Enable bilingual selection translation in the extension menu, select a word or passage, and click the nearby icon.

[Open guide](/en/guide/deepseek-harness)

## Hover translation

Hover over a paragraph and press **Control** to translate it.

[Open guide](/en/guide/hover-translation)

## AI reading card and learning center

The reading card can explain tone, unpack a long sentence, show usage, or suggest a practice question.

[Open guide](/en/guide/vocabulary-book)

## Images, files, and subtitles

- [Images and areas](/en/guide/image-translation): recognize and translate text in an image or screenshot.
- [Documents](/en/guide/document-translation): import, translate, edit, and download files.
- [Video and meeting captions](/en/guide/video-subtitles): supported platforms, caption sources, and display options.

## Your service and settings

Use the ready-to-use free service or connect a provider such as DeepL, an AI service, or a local Ollama model.

[Open guide](/en/config/translation-engines)

## More translation languages

Source and target selectors now offer 52 language options, including Simplified and Traditional Chinese, German, Portuguese, Italian, Arabic, Hindi, Vietnamese, Thai, Ukrainian, and Swahili.

[Open guide](/en/guide/webpage-translation)

## Bilingual share cards

Hover over a completed bilingual paragraph and choose **Create card**, or use **Create card** at the bottom of a selection translation result.

[Open guide](/en/guide/share-cards)
