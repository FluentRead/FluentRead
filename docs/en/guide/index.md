# What are you reading?

**Another language? Keep reading.**

FluentRead is an open-source browser translation extension. Read an article beside its translation, check a sentence where you found it, and save expressions you’d like to use yourself.

New here? Start with [Install & try it](/en/guide/getting-started). You can choose a different translation service later.

| What you have in front of you | Where to go |

| An article or webpage | [Webpage & quick translation](/en/guide/features) |
| A tricky sentence | [Reading card](/en/guide/deepseek-harness) |

## Make it comfortable

Choose languages, translation styles, automatic translation, and menu layout in [Settings](/en/config/). Use a [glossary](/en/guide/glossary) for names and specialized terms.

The free translation service is ready to use. To use your own provider or AI explanations, follow [Translation services](/en/config/translation-engines). Providers may charge separately.

Stuck? Try [Troubleshooting](/en/guide/faq). See [Data & privacy](/en/guide/privacy) for what stays local and what goes to a service.
