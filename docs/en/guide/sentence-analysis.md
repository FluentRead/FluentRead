## Use it on a webpage

1. Enable **Selection translation** and choose **Card view**.
2. Under **AI explanations**, connect an AI provider and model.
3. Select a sentence, open the card, and choose **Structure**.
4. Read the phrase labels. Choose a phrase, or use the left and right arrow keys, to inspect its explanation.

The top line keeps the original words in order. The second line displays sentence roles and parts of speech. These differ: a noun can be the subject of one sentence and the object of another.

## When labels are unavailable

Annotations are generated by your selected AI. If the response does not match the original words or uses an unsupported format, FluentRead keeps the plain-text explanation. Treat analysis as a learning reference.

## Requests and privacy

Opening the card does not request AI. Choosing a learning action sends the selection and only the context you permitted to the configured service.
